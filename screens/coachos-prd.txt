# CoachOS Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## Vision
"Manage 100 fitness clients as easily as managing 10." A platform focused on coach efficiency and client retention through AI-powered insights and rapid logging.

## Core User Personas
1. **The Coach:** High-volume professional needing a "command center" to spot clients needing attention.
2. **The Client:** Everyday user needing to log data in <30 seconds to maintain streaks.

## Key Modules & Workflows
- **Intelligence Dashboard (Coach):** Priority-based client management (Attention Required vs. Doing Great).
- **Client Profile & Analytics:** Deep dive into trends (Weight, Adherence, Sleep) with Before/After comparisons.
- **Rapid Check-in (Client Mobile):** 30-second daily logging flow.
- **Workout Builder & Logger:** Template-based program design and touch-friendly exercise logging.
- **AI Coach Assistant:** Proactive alerts on plateaus and suggestions for protocol adjustments.

## Design Language
- Dark mode, Minimalist, Data-dense but clear.
- Rounded corners, subtle glass effects.
- High-fidelity charts and streak indicators.
